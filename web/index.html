Framing guide

The model reads what is in the frame and invents what is cut off. Framing is your job; nothing here fixes a bad photo.

  • Fill the frame with the Nutrition Facts panel. Keep the whole table in view, edges included.
  • Flat and sharp. Press creased wrappers flat, avoid glare and shadow across the numbers.
  • Upright. A sideways photo makes the model loop. We rotate by EXIF, but check it.
  • One product per photo.

The first read after a quiet period can take two to three minutes while the model wakes up.
