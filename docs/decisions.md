- 2026-11-04 — OFF sample: search returns neither `image_nutrition_url` nor a nutrition entry in `selected_images`; the fetcher builds the URL from `images.nutrition_<lang>.rev` (`.400.jpg` swapped for `.full.jpg`). A product counts toward a language only if it has a nutrition photo tagged with that language; products with all-zero or <4 nutrient values or no energy are dropped (mineral water made "carbs = 0" a free pass). Heavy search pages 503; the fetcher retries with a smaller `page_size`.
- 2026-11-04 — OFF caveat: the per-image language tag is contributor-supplied and unreliable (the two "ja" items are a French chips pack and a Chinese vinegar with a Japanese importer sticker), and OFF nutriments can disagree with the label (sticker says 43 kcal, carbs 1.5 g; OFF says 32 kcal, 3.8 g). Language buckets are by OFF tag until assigned by eye; scores on OFF are against OFF, not against the pack.
- 2026-11-04 — Parser: one label-scan rule (first number after a label and before the next label, unit before or after) instead of per-layout regexes, because real output varies: one-line-per-nutrient, HTML tables with unit and value in separate cells, glued single-line blobs, and prose lines. A gram/mg value must carry a unit (a bare number is a calories or %DV column); an explicit kcal beats a kJ conversion; `verified` = panel parsed AND no cut-off flag, and the smoke test additionally requires the output not looped. The cut-off heuristic (`[…]`, trailing hyphen, unbalanced parenthesis) does NOT fire on the yogurt, whose ingredients are cut off but come back well-formed, so ingredients stay unverified text regardless.
